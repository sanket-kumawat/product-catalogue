# Log AI usage
Append to `AI_USAGE.md` under the right heading:
- **Key prompt/moment**: what I asked, what it produced, what changed as a result.
- **AI mistake**: what the AI got wrong, how it was caught (review, test,
  typecheck, manual run), and the fix.
Keep entries to 2-4 lines, factual, first person. Never invent entries; ask me
if unsure what happened.
